About This Site

Methodology

  1. Content sources: only publicly available current guidelines from international wound-care societies (WHS, IWGDF, EPUAP/NPIAP/PPPIA, WOCN, EWMA, ISTAP, ISBI, SVS) and the primary literature they cite.
  2. Citation verification: every journal citation is individually checked against the official Crossref DOI registry and PubMed (DOI, authors, journal, volume/issue/year); institutional documents are verified for existence on official websites. Verification date: 2026-08.
  3. Update mechanism: each page shows its last-updated date; when a guideline is revised, pages are reviewed and revised one by one and recorded in the version history.
  4. Translation principle: Chinese summaries prioritize fidelity to the source; grading tables are summarized rearrangements of the original — for clinical use, please read the full original guidelines alongside.

Brand neutrality & conflict-of-interest statement

This site accepts no sponsorship from any dressing manufacturer. The "brand × category cross-reference" is for product identification only and does not constitute a ranking or purchasing recommendation. Society guidelines recommend dressing categories; adoption of a specific product should consider, in order: independent society guidelines → independent systematic reviews / comparative trials → local formulary and regulatory approval → the manufacturer's current IFU.

Disclaimer

The content of this site is an educational summary of clinical guidelines and cannot replace professional assessment and judgment for individual patients. Clinical decisions must integrate the patient's condition, local regulations, insurance coverage, and institutional policy. Wound Tracker App: transfer case photos with a USB drive

Before you start: a USB-C drive plugs directly into the iPhone (older USB-A drives need an adapter); recommended format exFAT or FAT32; once plugged in, the drive appears in the iPhone Files app.

1. Upload (App → USB drive): on the Tracking tab tap the share icon (⬆) on the case → choose a format — encrypted package (.woundpkg) = full records + photos, password-protected, opened with the app (best for formal exchange and backup); plain viewing package (ZIP) = photos (incl. date-stamped copies) + PDF summary, viewable on any computer (for clinicians without the app; requires unencrypted-risk confirmation) → on the share sheet choose "Save to Files" → select the USB drive → Save. The export summary (file name + photo count + integrity verified) confirms success.

2. Download (USB drive → App): plug in the drive containing the .woundpkg → tap the download icon (⬇) at the top right of the Tracking tab → Browse → USB drive → select the file → enter the password → the app verifies integrity (8 checks) and imports it as a new case; existing data is never overwritten.

3. Notes: ① the plain ZIP is NOT encrypted — anyone can view the photos if the drive is lost; use the encrypted package for formal exchange ② passwords have no backdoor — if forgotten the package cannot be opened ③ exported photos have GPS/EXIF removed and use anonymous IDs ④ re-importing the same package is detected and flagged ⑤ moving to a new phone: export an encrypted package to the drive on the old phone → import from the drive on the new phone.
