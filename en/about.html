About This Site

Methodology

  1. Content sources: only publicly available current guidelines from international wound-care societies (WHS, IWGDF, EPUAP/NPIAP/PPPIA, WOCN, EWMA, ISTAP, ISBI, SVS) and the primary literature they cite.
  2. Citation verification: every journal citation is individually checked against the official Crossref DOI registry and PubMed (DOI, authors, journal, volume/issue/year); institutional documents are verified for existence on official websites. Verification date: 2026-08.
  3. Update mechanism: each page shows its last-updated date; when a guideline is revised, pages are reviewed and revised one by one and recorded in the version history.
  4. Translation principle: Chinese summaries prioritize fidelity to the source; grading tables are summarized rearrangements of the original — for clinical use, please read the full original guidelines alongside.

Brand neutrality & conflict-of-interest statement

This site accepts no sponsorship from any dressing manufacturer. The "brand × category cross-reference" is for product identification only and does not constitute a ranking or purchasing recommendation. Society guidelines recommend dressing categories; adoption of a specific product should consider, in order: independent society guidelines → independent systematic reviews / comparative trials → local formulary and regulatory approval → the manufacturer's current IFU.

Disclaimer

The content of this site is an educational summary of clinical guidelines and cannot replace professional assessment and judgment for individual patients. Clinical decisions must integrate the patient's condition, local regulations, insurance coverage, and institutional policy. This site assumes no legal liability for any direct or indirect consequences arising from use of its content.
