WIfI / SINBAD / UT Calculators

⚠️ These tools are for teaching and clinical decision support only; results cannot replace a complete clinical assessment and professional judgment.

SINBAD Calculator (Diabetic Foot Ulcer)

Six items scored 0–1 each, total 0–6. Used for communication, audit, and prognostic stratification (the basic classification recommended by IWGDF 2023).

S — Site
I — Ischemia
N — Neuropathy
B — Bacterial infection
A — Area
D — Depth
Source: Monteiro-Soares M, et al. IWGDF 2023 classification update. DOI:10.1002/dmrr.3648

WIfI Calculator (SVS Threatened Limb Classification)

Wound / Ischemia / foot Infection each graded 0–3 → estimates the 1-year amputation-risk stage and benefit of revascularization (SVS 2014).

W — Wound
I — Ischemia (ABI / toe pressure / TcPO₂, whichever available)
fI — foot Infection (IWGDF/IDSA correspondence)
Source: Mills JL Sr, et al. SVS WIfI. J Vasc Surg. 2014;59(1):220–234. DOI:10.1016/j.jvs.2013.08.003 (stage lookup summarized from Table 6 of the original article)

University of Texas (UT) Classifier (Diabetic Foot Ulcer)

A 4×4 matrix of depth Grade 0–3 × infection/ischemia Stage A–D (1996). Deeper wounds with infection/ischemia carry higher amputation risk. See Staging & Records, section 4.

Grade — depth (0–3)
Stage — infection and ischemia (A–D)
Source: Lavery LA, Armstrong DG, Harkless LB. 1996 (DOI:10.1016/s1067-2516(96)80125-6); Armstrong DG, et al. Diabetes Care 1998;21(5):855–859 (DOI:10.2337/diacare.21.5.855) (validation study: depth and infection/ischemia each increase amputation risk)
