Core principles (site-wide)

  • Treat the cause first, then choose the dressing: offload the diabetic foot, compress the venous leg, revascularize the arterial limb, redistribute pressure for pressure injuries — the dressing is a supporting actor.
  • Do not give antibiotics to uninfected ulcers (IWGDF/IDSA 2023).
  • Offloading cannot be replaced by any dressing (IWGDF Offloading 2023).
  • Guidelines recommend dressing "categories", not brands; evidence hierarchy: society guidelines → systematic reviews → local formulary → manufacturer IFU.
  • Advanced therapies (NPWT / oxygen / cellular-matrix products) only after standard care is optimized (IWGDF 2023).
