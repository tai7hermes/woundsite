3. Verification Statement

All journal references listed above were checked item by item in 2026-08 against the official Crossref DOI registry and PubMed: DOIs, authors, journals, and volume/issue/year were all consistent, and no fabricated (hallucinated) references were found. The single correction: the journal originally cited for Van Tiggelen 2020 was corrected from International Wound Journal to British Journal of Dermatology. On 2026-08-29, 10 references on wound assessment & documentation systems were added (34–43): 6 journal articles verified via Crossref/PubMed, 4 from official institutional sources (NPIAP, JSPU, IWGDF). On 2026-08-31, 4 wound-infection references were added (44–47): IWII 2022 verified via PubMed (PMID:36475844) and the official publication page; NICE NG152 and CDC are official institutional guidelines; IWGDF/IDSA matches existing ref 43. The full verification report is archived in this site's methodology documentation.
