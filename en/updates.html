2. Site announcements

(Showing the three most recent updates only)

2026-08-31
Treatment & Alerts: new "Part 4: Wound Infection": IWII 2022 five-stage infection continuum, four groups of signs (classic / subtle-chronic / spreading / systemic), clinical diagnosis and culture sampling principles (incl. what NOT to do), blood tests and imaging tables, four treatment pillars (source control, local care, systemic antibiotics, etiology), 48–72 h follow-up, antimicrobial stewardship and emergency red flags; the safety checklist moves to Part 5; Guidelines Library adds refs 44–47 (IWII / IWGDF-IDSA / NICE NG152 / CDC, verified).
2026-08-29
Staging Systems: new "Wound Assessment & Documentation Systems" section: full coverage of TIME / TIMERS / MEASURE frameworks (tables + correct positioning), Wound Triangle / WBP 2021 / DIME / MOIST comparison, PUSH / BWAT / DESIGN-R quantitative tools with licensing notes, etiology-specific classification mapping, recommended documentation workflow and area-reduction formula; the Guideline Library adds 10 verified references on this topic (34–43, Crossref/PubMed checked).
2026-08-23
Dressing selection flowchart added: the professional "Dressings & Wet Gauze" page (bedside mnemonic and selection workflow) and the patient page of the same name (which dressing fits which wound) now include a "Wound Dressing Selection" decision flowchart — safety first (infection / ischemia / deep wounds need medical care), then four exudate levels (dry-minimal → hydrogel; low → film, hydrocolloid; moderate → foam, hydrofiber; heavy/cavity → alginate, superabsorbent) while addressing the underlying cause; each language edition uses an image in its own language.

3. How the tracking works

  • Frequency: automatic check of each society's official website and journal pages on the 1st of every month.
  • Comparison baseline: the "Version used on this site" column above.
  • When a new edition is detected: the site maintainer is notified → the new content is verified → affected pages are assessed → revisions are made and announced on this page.
  • Verification rule: every new citation is verified against Crossref / PubMed before going live.
