3. How the tracking works

  • Frequency: automatic check of each society's official website and journal pages on the 1st of every month.
  • Comparison baseline: the "Version used on this site" column above.
  • When a new edition is detected: the site maintainer (Tai7Hermes) is notified → the new content is verified → affected pages are assessed → revisions are made and announced on this page.
  • Verification rule: every new citation is verified against Crossref / PubMed before going live.
